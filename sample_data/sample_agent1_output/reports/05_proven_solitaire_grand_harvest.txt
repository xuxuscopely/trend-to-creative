Creative analysis — Solitaire Grand Harvest (sgh-win-004)

Much calmer pacing than the puzzle-blast creatives. Opens on a sun-drenched farm scene with
a solitaire card tableau overlaid; a player clears a run of cards in a smooth, unhurried
sequence (cuts every 2-3s). Each completed card run triggers a small sparkle effect and a
section of a farm/orchard visibly growing or repairing in the background — the "reward" is
visual progress on a cozy world rather than an explosive combo. Soft acoustic background
music, no voiceover, gentle text call-outs ("So relaxing!"). Ends on a wide shot of the
fully-restored farm scene and a "Play Free" CTA. Emotional register is "relaxing satisfaction
through completion," distinct from the chain-reaction-blast or competitive-rivalry patterns
seen elsewhere in this set.
