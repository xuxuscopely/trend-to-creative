Creative analysis — Coin Master (cm-spin-002)

Different creative execution, same app/campaign family as cm-spin-010. Opens on the spin
wheel landing on "Attack" rather than "Raid," cutting to the player's viking smashing a
pig-shaped piggy bank structure for a coin payout instead of a village wall. Keeps the same
competitive framing device — a banner naming a specific rival player — and the same
coin-pour payoff animation and "Raid Now" style CTA. Pacing and tone are consistent with the
other Coin Master creative: triumphant brass audio stings, cartoon-violence slapstick visual
humor, rivalry-driven narrative rather than pure visual satisfaction. Reads as a variant/retest
of the same creative concept rather than a distinct new idea.
