Creative analysis — Gardenscapes (gs-makeover-007)

Hybrid structure: opens with ~4 seconds of match-3 chain-reaction gameplay (similar cascading
combo style to Candy Crush/Toon Blast, slightly slower cuts), then transitions to a
"before/after" home makeover reveal — the match-3 win is shown funding a visible renovation
of a run-down garden area into a polished, colorful scene. The makeover reveal is the real
emotional payoff, with a slow push-in camera move and a satisfied "Wow!" text call-out rather
than a chain-reaction climax. Combines the puzzle-satisfaction hook from the blast games with
a longer-form "transformation" narrative more common in simulation/decor games. Ends on a
split-screen before/after shot and a "Restore the Garden" CTA.
