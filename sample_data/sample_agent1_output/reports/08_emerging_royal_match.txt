Creative analysis — Royal Match (rm-001)

Opens with a UGC-style "phone recording a phone screen" framing device — looks like a real
person filming themselves playing, shaky handheld camera, informal lighting. The player taps
through a match-3 board while a friend's voice (off-screen, captioned) taunts them for being
behind in the shared Kingdom restoration event. Mid-video cuts to a direct-comparison split
screen: "Your Kingdom" (half-built, drab) vs "Friend's Kingdom" (fully restored, vibrant),
explicitly framing the game as a social status competition rather than pure puzzle
satisfaction. Captions throughout mimic casual texting style ("bro really thinks he's
winning 💀"). Ends on a "Catch Up Now" CTA. This creative is brand new (first seen ~1 week
ago per Agent 1 metadata) and is testing a distinctly different hook — social-comparison/
rivalry framed through fake-UGC — compared to Royal Match's older, more conventional
gameplay-forward creatives.
