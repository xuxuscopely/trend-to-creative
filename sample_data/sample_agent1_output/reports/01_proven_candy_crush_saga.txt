Creative analysis — Candy Crush Saga (ccs-combo-005)

Opens cold on a near-full board mid-combo: a single swipe triggers a cascading chain of
candy matches that fills the entire screen within 2 seconds, each match punctuated by a
rising chime and screen-shake. No logo, no VO for the first 3 seconds — the chain reaction
itself is the hook. Mid-section cuts to a "almost lost, saved by one lucky swipe" beat with
a red countdown timer, building tension before the payoff. Editing is fast (cuts every
0.5-1s during the combo), bright oversaturated colors, large bold number counters ("+4,500!")
flying off-screen. Ends on a UI shot of the level-complete screen with confetti and a
"Play Now" CTA button overlay. No dialogue or narration anywhere in the 24s — it's 100%
visual, sound-design-led satisfaction.
