Creative analysis — Block Blast! (al-bb-019)

Follows the same chain-reaction-blast template as the Candy Crush/Toon Blast creatives: opens
mid-combo on a nearly-full grid, one placement triggers a cascading line-clear combo across
most of the board within 2 seconds, escalating "Nice! Great! Amazing!" text stacking, fast
cuts, bright saturated colors, triumphant number counters. No voiceover. Tension beat (board
almost full, one good move left) before the payoff clear. Ends on a "Play Now" CTA over a
high-score graphic. This is a newer creative on its network but is executing an already-
proven structural template rather than testing something novel — further evidence the
chain-reaction-combo hook is a durable, widely-reused pattern rather than a one-off.
